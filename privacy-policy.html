FocusForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

ZUNVERO LABS ("we", "us", or "our") operates the FocusForge Android application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use FocusForge.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, FocusForge may process:

  • Account information: If you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and account identifier provided by Google.
  • Study information: Subjects, topics, study goals, study sessions, quiz history, scores, XP, achievements, streaks, progress, and related preferences.
  • Photos or study materials: If you voluntarily upload or capture study-related photos or materials using features provided by the App.
  • Device permissions: The App may request access to the camera or photos only when a feature requires it and you grant the relevant permission.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate FocusForge features.
  • Save and display your study progress.
  • Maintain study sessions, achievements, streaks, and quiz results.
  • Provide personalized study-related features.
  • Synchronize your data across supported devices.
  • Improve reliability, security, and functionality of the App.

3. AI Processing

Some FocusForge features may use artificial intelligence to process information related to your study sessions, topics, questions, answers, or study materials in order to provide AI-powered study functionality.

Information sent to an AI service is used only as necessary to provide the requested feature and according to the applicable service's terms and privacy practices.

4. Cloud Backup and Synchronization

If cloud backup or synchronization is enabled in the App, relevant account and study-progress information may be stored using cloud services so that supported data can be restored or accessed across supported devices.

5. Offline Use

Some FocusForge features may work without an internet connection. Information created while using offline features may remain stored locally until synchronization is available, depending on the feature.

6. Advertising and Analytics

FocusForge does not intentionally display third-party advertising or use third-party advertising SDKs.

We do not intentionally use analytics for advertising or cross-app behavioral profiling.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are required to operate specific FocusForge features, such as authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, or AI-powered functionality. Such processing is limited to the purposes necessary to provide those services.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by FocusForge against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide FocusForge features, maintain your account, provide synchronization, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

If you want to request deletion of information associated with your FocusForge account, contact us using the email address below.

10. Children's Privacy

FocusForge is intended to provide educational and productivity features for learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us improperly by a child, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to FocusForge, our services, or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
